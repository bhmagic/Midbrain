# Action: lay gripped object flat

This finite reverse-pickup Skill uses the two-vector effector profile to perform one Slicing-style Integrated rotation-only alignment, verifies `FLOAT`, hands off through Integrated `WARM` with no Basic lease, and commands an absolute-root combined table-inward and negative-insertion Contact move. It releases, transitions the gripper to MIT float, retreats farther against insertion with a measured-start-relative Contact move, and then relaxes the arm.

The Skill owns separate gripper-vector and motion profiles plus its `.venv`. Its developer UI exposes both gripper directions and both object/world directions, profile selection, typed overrides, and four attended stages matching the regular controller sequence. Its approach-begin entry may be an absolute active-world point or a world-axis offset from the current controlled effector; relative mode captures and freezes the Integrated measured origin during preparation. Stage 1 rotates through Integrated only. Stage 2 submits an `ABSOLUTE_ROOT` Contact placement target composed from the frozen approach point and the table/negative-insertion offsets. Stage 3 opens and floats the gripper, and Stage 4 retreats relatively through Contact before relaxation.

Lay Flat starts with a confirmed carry, so its Integrated rotation and `FLOAT` handoff are an explicit exception to the normal uninterrupted Contact `POSITION_EFFORT_LIMITED` carry rule. The regular and developer paths expose this exception instead of claiming all joints remain POS_TOR during Stage 1. After handoff, Contact owns placement, the arm hold during release, and retreat. The gripper enters MIT float only after measured opening.
