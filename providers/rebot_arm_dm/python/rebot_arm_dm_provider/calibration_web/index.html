Manual joint testing

Raw velocity mode is intentionally unavailable. Each slider is a pointer deadman. Press and drag with the mouse or touch; releasing anywhere immediately enters gravity-float and snaps the target back to the measured angle.

Use a mechanically supported arm for first hardware motion. Start with very small ranges. Low-kp MIT is prohibited. MIT spring stiffness uses the supplied working defaults; velocity damping may be low. POS_VEL and FORCE_POS remain velocity/torque capped by the provider.

Automatic friction calibration

Move the arm by hand in gravity-float to a clear pose. The factory mass, center-of-mass, inertia, and gravity model remain unchanged. All seven joints remain in POSITION_VELOCITY_LIMITED mode: six hold the captured pose while one performs two forward/reverse traverses at two speeds to estimate Coulomb and viscous friction. MIT is not used during automatic calibration.

Workspace confirmation required. This utility detects only simplified robot self-collision and the configured desktop plane. It cannot detect people, monitors, walls, lamps, shelves, tools, cables, or any other external obstacle.
