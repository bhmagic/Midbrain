# Notice

Integrated 0.8.0 is a physical arm/gripper bring-up prototype, not the final generalized arm planner.

Its purpose is to validate Cartesian movement through ONE_SHOT/HOLD_LB MIT control, latched POS_VEL transit, baseline-budgeted short-stroke POS_TOR work, selectable gripper MIT/POS_TOR control, 3-DoF/6-DoF IK, tool acting-point offsets, payload-aware gravity feed-forward, Fabric target staging, and explicit return to gravity-float before obstacle avoidance and Cartesian force estimation are completed.
